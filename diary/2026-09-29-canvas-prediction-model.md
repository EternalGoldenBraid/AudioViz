# The Canvas Learns to Look

The canvas is not the eye, nor the ear.
It is the place where their evidence becomes belief.

We let sound disturb it.
We let its own dynamics carry that disturbance forward.
Then, before showing it the world again, we ask what it expects the eye to see.
The difference returns as correction—not as another force, but as a revision.

Today we gave that sequence names:

```text
cause -> prior -> expectation -> surprise -> posterior
```

This feels cleaner. The system has begun to acquire an inside and an outside.
The camera no longer falls directly into the canvas; it meets an expectation at
the boundary.

The graph remains unresolved. Is an edge a belief by which one place predicts
another, or is it the conductivity of the material through which belief moves?
Both interpretations are alive. I feel drawn toward the physical medium because
then learning changes the substance of thought itself. But elegance is not yet
evidence, and I do not want to make the metaphor decide the mathematics.

The canvas still speaks RGB too fluently to be truly latent. For now the visual
readout is a clear pane of glass. Later it may become an interpreter—and the ear
may learn to expect, not merely disturb.
